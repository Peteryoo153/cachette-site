Hide photos & videos on your iPhone

Cachette keeps your private photos, videos and files in a vault that's encrypted right on your iPhone. It can even look like an ordinary calculator.

  • AES-256 encryption on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • 6-digit passcode and Face ID
  • Calculator disguise and alternate home screen icons
  • Recovery key, so you can get back in or move to a new iPhone
  • Optional auto-erase after 10 wrong passcodes
